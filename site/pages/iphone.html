Hopper for iPhone

Your Mac's Hopper, in your pocket: see what's running and what's waiting on you, answer Claude, start new work and move it along. The work still happens on your Mac; the phone is a window onto it.

What you need

  • Hopper on a Mac, version 0.8.0 or later, with Claude Code signed in. Install it with 
    curl -fsSL https://hopper.saltbark.com/install.sh | sh
    or update it with hopper update.
  • An iPhone on iOS 27 or later.

Pair your phone

  1. On your Mac, run hopper pair. It shows a code that works once, for five minutes.
  2. Open the app on your phone and point the camera at the code.
  3. Say yes on the Mac when it asks whether to let your phone in.

That's all. The phone connects whenever Hopper is open on the Mac. To see paired phones on the Mac, run hopper phones; to forget one, hopper unpair <name>. On the phone, unpair from Accounts.

When something's wrong

"Mac away"
Hopper isn't open on your Mac, or the Mac is asleep. The phone shows what it last heard and connects again by itself once Hopper is back. Press z in Hopper to keep the Mac awake while you're away.
"Update needed"
The app and Hopper on your Mac are on different versions. The app says which one to update: the app from the App Store, or Hopper with hopper update.
"Pair again"
The relay no longer knows this pairing, or it was made by an older version. Unpair in Accounts, run hopper pair on the Mac and scan the new code.
"Offline"
The phone can't reach the relay. It keeps trying; tap the status to try at once.
The code won't scan
Make the terminal window bigger so the code is sharp, or copy the link under it on the Mac and use "Paste the link instead" on the phone.

Privacy

No accounts, nothing collected. Everything between your phone and your Mac is encrypted end to end; the relay in between can't read it. The details are in the privacy policy.
